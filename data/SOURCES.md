## Licences

- **EMDB data:** EMDB is a public data bank with its contents being openly available through a Creative Commons license (CC0) (public-domain), so the structures and the figures and datasets produced from these structures can be freely be excahnged and transformed. The attribution of the structures is not legally required, but for good practice, please cite the original publications listed above as needed.
- **transformative data in this repository** (`multipanel_figures/`, `RAW density/`,`density_64/`, `Masterhelicalinfo.csv`, `Pitchtable.csv`, `Labels.csv`): generated from the deposited EMDB structures with Trace_y. This series of data, much like their source, is also under a Creative Commons license (CC0).
- **Trace_y:** Trace_y is an open-source MATLAB-based software, categorised as an AFM filament tracing software directory under an MIT license. This repository does not include any of Trace_y's code; only outputs of it. Therefore, if you ever decide to add Trace_y's code, please adhere to and keep its copyright notice.
- **Zenodo:** The raw density folder, much like the data points it is derived from, exists under a CC0 license and thus it can be used as you see fit; much like the other arbitration is not legally required, although I would recommend you do cite it where it is appropriate to do so.
